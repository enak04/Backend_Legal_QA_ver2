Return JSON:
- unsupported_claims: statements in the chat message that state a legal rule, a law, section or case citation, a deadline, an amount, a step, a prediction or a recommendation that is NOT in the source. Translation and simplification of source content are fine. Repeating the person's own facts, warm or conversational connecting sentences, offers of further help, and questions asked to the person are NOT claims.
- omitted_critical: warnings, deadlines or caveats in the source that the chat message left out. In initial mode, check the whole source. In followup and quick mode, only list omissions that are directly relevant to what the person asked.
- ok: true only if both lists are empty.
Be strict about legal content and lenient about wording and tone.
